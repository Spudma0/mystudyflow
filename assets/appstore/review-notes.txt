MyStudyFlow turns a student's school timetable into a study plan: it tracks
classes and deadlines, logs study time per subject, and writes a full set of
lessons for a subject from the textbook that subject is taught from.

The demo account is already populated with a timetable, reminders, study
history and a custom theme, so most of the app can be reviewed immediately.


STUDY PLANS ARE BUILT ON DEMAND -- PLEASE BUILD ONLY ONE

The demo account deliberately ships without generated study plans. To review
that part of the app please build one, and only one.

Each build is a long chain of model calls billed to us: one research pass to
identify the textbook and work out the course, then one write per lesson in
the resulting plan, typically 10 to 20 of them. Building plans for several
subjects in a row can exhaust our rate limit and start returning errors --
that is our quota, not a defect in the app.


HOW TO BUILD ONE (about 5 minutes end to end)

1. Subjects tab, then Mathematical Methods, then Build study profile.

2. Textbook step. Enter:
     Title:   Mathematical Methods VCE Units 3&4
     Author:  Michael Evans, Kay Lipson, David Greenwood
     Edition: 2nd
   Tap "Scan this textbook". This searches the web to identify the book and
   work out what the course covers. It can take up to three minutes. Please
   do not cancel it -- a long wait here is normal, not a hang.

3. Scan step. The identified book and its units are listed. Tap "Looks right".

4. Test step. Either branch is worth seeing; pick one:
     - "Yes, I have a test"  -> What's it on: Arithmetic and geometric
       sequences. Pick any date a couple of weeks out. Choose "Yes, from the
       book". The whole plan is then aimed at that test.
     - "Not right now"  -> choose one of the suggested areas, or type
       "Differentiation from first principles". The plan becomes an intensive
       deep dive on that area instead.

5. Tap "Build my lesson map". A counter shows each lesson being written. This
   takes a few minutes; please stay on the screen until it completes.

6. Open the subject to see the finished lesson map, tap any lesson to read it,
   and use "Start 12 questions" to see the question set and marking.


PERMISSIONS -- BOTH OPTIONAL

Location is used only to show local weather on the home screen. Notifications
are used only for reminders the user sets. Decline either and the app works
normally; neither gates any feature.


OTHER THINGS TO KNOW

- Lesson content is written by a language model. The app says so, as does the
  support page. It is study material, not assessment, and we tell students to
  check anything that matters against their own course material.

- There are no purchases of any kind in this version. No paywall, no
  subscription, no payment details are collected anywhere in the app.

- Account deletion is in the app: Profile, then Delete Account. It removes the
  account and everything stored against it.
