PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
A study planner for secondary students (15-18) working toward end-of-school
exams, whose timetable, deadlines and actual studying normally live in three
unconnected places. It reads the school timetable in from a photo or PDF,
tracks assignments and exams with reminders and countdowns, times study
sessions and logs them against the right subject, and builds an ordered set of
lessons for a subject from the textbook it is taught from.

ACCESS
The demo account is pre-loaded with a timetable, reminders, study history and
a theme, so most of the app can be reviewed at once. No sample files needed.

STUDY PLANS ARE BUILT ON DEMAND -- PLEASE BUILD ONLY ONE
The demo account ships without generated plans so the generation flow can be
seen. Each build is a research pass plus one model call per lesson (10-20),
billed to us; several in a row can exhaust our rate limit and start erroring.
That is our quota, not a defect.

To build one (about 3 minutes):
1. Subjects tab > Mathematical Methods > Build study profile.
2. Textbook step. Title: Cambridge Maths Methods Units 1 And 2
   Leave author and edition blank -- this is the book the demo account's own
   plan was built from, so its lessons are already in our shared cache and
   come back in seconds. Tap "Scan this textbook".
3. Scan step: the book and its units are listed. Tap "Looks right".
4. Test step: "Yes, I have a test"; what it is on: Calculus; any date in
   December; "Yes, from the book".
5. Tap "Build my lesson map" and stay on the screen while it writes.
6. Open the subject for the lesson map, tap a lesson, then "Start 12
   questions".

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Supabase - authentication and storage of the student's own data.
Anthropic (Claude) - reads imported timetable images, researches the named
  textbook, writes lessons and questions. Our requests carry no user
  identifier and no account details.
Open-Meteo - weather for the optional clock widget; coordinates rounded to
  about 1 km, no account details.
Railway - hosts our backend.  Expo (EAS) - builds and app updates.
No ad networks, analytics SDKs, payment processors, data brokers or
tracking.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. No geographic gating, no region-specific content, no locale-dependent
behaviour. English only. The one location-aware feature is the optional
weather widget, which behaves the same everywhere.

REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry: no medical, financial, legal or professional advice,
not an accredited provider, examination board or credential issuer, no claims
about grades. No textbook is hosted, copied or distributed. A named textbook
is identified from public information and only its bibliographic details and
chapter and topic names are reported; our instruction to the model forbids
reproducing teaching text, worked examples, exercises or any substantial
passage. Lessons are written from scratch against a topic name with no access
to the book, so they are original and need no publisher licence. We claim no
affiliation with any publisher.

PERMISSIONS -- BOTH OPTIONAL
Location only for home screen weather; notifications only for reminders the
user sets. Decline either and the app works normally.
