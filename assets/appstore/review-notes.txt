PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
MyStudyFlow is a study planner for secondary students (15-18) working toward
end-of-school exams. Their timetable, deadlines and actual studying normally
live in three unconnected places. The app reads the school timetable in from a
photo or PDF, tracks assignments and exams with reminders and countdowns,
times study sessions and logs them against the right subject, and builds an
ordered set of lessons for a subject from the textbook it is taught from.

ACCESS
The demo account is pre-loaded with a timetable, reminders, study history and
a theme, so most of the app can be reviewed immediately. No sample files are
needed.

STUDY PLANS ARE BUILT ON DEMAND -- PLEASE BUILD ONLY ONE
The demo account deliberately ships without generated plans so the generation
flow can be seen. Each build is a research pass plus one model call per lesson
(10-20), billed to us; several in a row can exhaust our rate limit and start
erroring. That is our quota, not a defect.

To build one (about 5 minutes):
1. Subjects tab > Mathematical Methods > Build study profile.
2. Textbook step:
     Title:   Mathematical Methods VCE Units 3&4
     Author:  Michael Evans, Kay Lipson, David Greenwood
     Edition: 2nd
   Tap "Scan this textbook". This searches the web and can take up to three
   minutes. A long wait here is expected, not a hang.
3. Scan step: the book and its units are listed. Tap "Looks right".
4. Test step, either branch:
     "Yes, I have a test" -> What's it on: Arithmetic and geometric sequences;
     any date a fortnight out; "Yes, from the book".
     "Not right now" -> pick one of the suggested areas.
5. Tap "Build my lesson map" and stay on the screen while it writes.
6. Open the subject for the lesson map, tap a lesson, then "Start 12
   questions" for the question set.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Supabase - authentication and storage of the student's own data.
Anthropic (Claude) - reads imported timetable images, researches the named
  textbook, writes lessons and questions. Our requests carry no user
  identifier and no account details.
Open-Meteo - weather for the optional clock widget. Coordinates rounded to
  about 1 km, no account details.
Railway - hosts our backend.  Expo (EAS) - builds and app updates.
No ad networks, no analytics SDKs, no payment processors, no data brokers,
no tracking.

REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. No geographic gating, no region-specific content, no locale-dependent
behaviour. English only. The only location-aware feature is the optional
weather widget, which behaves the same everywhere.

REGULATED INDUSTRY AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not a regulated industry: no medical, financial, legal or professional advice,
not an accredited provider, examination board or credential issuer, no claims
about grades. No textbook is hosted, copied or distributed. A named textbook
is identified from public information and only factual bibliographic details
and its chapter and topic names are reported; our instruction to the model
forbids reproducing teaching text, worked examples, exercises or any
substantial passage. Lessons are then written from scratch against a topic
name with no access to the book, so they are original material and need no
publisher licence. We claim no affiliation with any publisher.

PERMISSIONS -- BOTH OPTIONAL
Location only for weather on the home screen; notifications only for reminders
the user sets. Decline either and the app works normally.
