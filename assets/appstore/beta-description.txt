MyStudyFlow turns your school timetable into a study plan.

It holds your classes, your deadlines and your actual studying in one place,
and then writes you a full set of lessons for a subject from the textbook that
subject is taught from.

WHAT IS IN THIS BUILD

Timetable — photograph your timetable or upload the PDF and it is read in for
you, cycle and all, whether your school runs a five-day week or a ten-day
fortnight. Or build it by hand. Today is one tap away, with room and teacher
on every class and a live marker showing where you are in the day. On iPad you
get the full week and the whole month at once.

Reminders — assignments, personal tasks and exams, each with a notification
that actually arrives. Exams count down to the minute.

Study sessions — start a session, say what you are working on, set a finish
time, add breaks on a repeating interval if you want them. The timer fills the
screen. When you finish, the time lands against that subject on its own.

Statistics — where your hours actually went, per subject and per week, and a
rank for each subject that climbs from Wood to Gold as you put time in.

Study plans — name the textbook your class uses and the app works out what
your course covers, builds an ordered map of lessons, and writes each one:
real explanation, worked examples, properly typeset maths, drawn chemical
structures, and a question set that marks itself.

Appearance — accent, background and card colours are all yours to pick, and
the widgets on the home screen can be chosen and reordered.

Everything follows your account between devices.

WHAT IS WORTH TRYING

1. Import your real timetable from a photo and see how much it gets right.
   This is the feature most likely to meet a layout it has not seen, so an
   odd result here is genuinely useful to know about.
2. Set a reminder with a notification and check it arrives.
3. Run a study session, including the breaks, and see the time logged.
4. Build a study plan for one subject, then read a lesson end to end and run
   its questions.
5. Change the theme and reorder the home screen widgets.
6. If you have an iPad, look at the week and month views, in both
   orientations.

PLEASE BUILD ONLY ONE OR TWO STUDY PLANS

Each plan is a research pass over your textbook plus a written lesson for
every topic in it, usually ten to twenty of them. They take a few minutes and
they cost us real money per build, so please do not build plans for every
subject. If one starts failing, that is us hitting a rate limit rather than
a bug in the app.

KNOWN LIMITS

- Lessons are written by a language model. They can be wrong. Check anything
  that matters against your own textbook or your teacher.
- The textbook scan is only as good as what is publicly documented about your
  book. Widely used textbooks come back in much more detail than obscure ones.
- English only.
- Nothing costs money in this build. There are no purchases and no paywall.
- Location is only ever used for the weather on the home screen, and
  notifications only for reminders you set. Decline either and everything
  else still works.
