MyStudyFlow keeps your whole school week in one place — and then helps you actually study.

YOUR TIMETABLE, THE WAY YOUR SCHOOL RUNS IT
Photograph your timetable or upload the PDF and it is read in for you, cycle and all — a normal five-day week or a ten-day fortnight. Or build it by hand in a couple of minutes. Today is one tap away, with the room and teacher on every class and a live marker showing exactly where you are in the day. On iPad you also get the full week and the whole month at once.

EVERY DEADLINE, COUNTED DOWN
Assignments, personal tasks and exams, each with a reminder that actually arrives. Exams get a countdown to the minute, so the test three weeks out stops being a surprise. Tick things off and they move out of the way.

STUDY SESSIONS THAT LOG THEMSELVES
Start a session, say what you are working on, set a finish time, and add breaks on a repeating interval if you want them. The timer fills the screen, so the phone stops being the thing distracting you. When you finish, the time lands against that subject on its own — nothing to type in afterwards.

SEE WHERE THE HOURS ACTUALLY GO
Your total, a breakdown by subject, and the week drawn out. Subjects climb through ranks as you put the work in, and a streak counts every day you study — the kind of number you do not want to break.

STUDY PLANS BUILT FROM YOUR OWN TEXTBOOK
Say which textbook you are using and what is coming up, and MyStudyFlow builds a plan from that book's real contents, then writes each lesson out in full:

• explanations broken into sections, with the key points pulled out
• core terms, and the formulas worth memorising, properly typeset
• worked examples with every step shown
• the mistakes people actually make, and how the topic gets examined
• twelve multiple-choice questions, easiest to hardest, each one explained
• graphs drawn for you, and skeletal structures for organic chemistry

Lessons are written once per textbook topic and shared, so the plan builds quickly and nothing is generated twice.

MAKE IT LOOK LIKE YOURS
Pick your accent, background and card colours, and the faint line pattern behind every screen. Choose which widgets sit at the top of your home screen and in what order: a clock with the weather where you are, an analog dial, the date, your checklist, your study streak, a countdown to your next exam, or how many classes are left today.

PHONE AND IPAD
Everything lives on your account, so your timetable, reminders, study history and colours follow you from one device to the next. The iPad layout uses the extra room rather than stretching the phone one.

YOUR DATA
Delete your account, and everything stored with it, from inside the app. No hunting through a website to do it.
