MyStudyFlow Support

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Something not working, or a question about how the app does what it does? Write to us and we will get back to you.

We aim to reply within two business days. It helps enormously if you include the device you are on, the iOS version, and what you were doing when it went wrong.

Common questions

My timetable did not import correctly

The importer reads a photo or a PDF and works out the cycle, but an unusual layout can confuse it. Everything it produces is editable: open Timetable, tap Edit, and fix any class by hand. If it reads your school's timetable badly, send us the file and we will look at why.

A lesson says it has no content

Lessons are written when the study plan is built. If one is empty, rebuild the plan from the subject's profile and the lessons will be written out in full. This needs an internet connection.

My data is missing on a new device

Your timetable, reminders, study history and colours live on your account. Sign in with the same email on the new device and they will come down. Data created while signed out stays on the device it was made on.

Reminders are not notifying me

Notifications are controlled by iOS, not by the app. Open Profile → Notifications, which takes you to MyStudyFlow's page in Settings, and check that Allow Notifications is on.

How do I delete my account?

Open Profile and tap Delete Account. That removes your account and everything stored against it. It cannot be undone. See the privacy policy for exactly what is held and for how long.

How do I change the app's colours?

Profile → Theme. Accent, background and card colours are all yours to pick, and they follow your account to your other devices. The widgets at the top of the home screen can be chosen and reordered by holding one down.

Reporting a problem

Tell us what you expected to happen and what happened instead. A screenshot is worth a paragraph. If the app closed unexpectedly, the time it happened helps us find it in the logs.

MyStudyFlow writes lessons with the help of a language model. It can be wrong, and it is not a substitute for your textbook or your teacher — always check anything that matters against your course material.
