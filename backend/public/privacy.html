MyStudyFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

MyStudyFlow is a study planner for students. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, where it goes, and how to get rid of it. It covers the MyStudyFlow iOS app and the service behind it.

What we collect

Account details

When you create an account we store your email address and a password. The password is hashed by our authentication provider and is never visible to us. During setup you may also give a name, school and year level, and the colours you pick for the app's appearance.

Your study data

Everything you put into the app is stored against your account so it is there on your other devices:

  • Your timetable — class names, rooms, teachers and times
  • Reminders, assignments and exams, including their titles, subjects and due dates
  • Study sessions — when you studied, for how long, and for which subject
  • Study plans, lesson progress and any questions you save for review
  • Textbook details you enter (title, author, edition)

Approximate location

Only if you allow it. The home screen's clock widget can show current weather, which needs a rough idea of where you are. Your coordinates are rounded to about one kilometre before they leave the device and are sent to a weather service to get the forecast. We do not store your location, and refusing the permission only means the widget shows a plain sun or moon instead.

Photos and files you choose to import

If you import a timetable, the photo or PDF you pick is sent to our server and on to our AI provider so the classes can be read out of it. It is processed in memory and discarded — we do not keep the image. The app only ever accesses the specific file you select; it does not browse your library.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, ad networks or advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs — we do not profile you or follow you across apps
  • We do not sell or rent your data, ever
  • Our server does not log the contents of your requests — only counts of how much text was processed

Who else handles your data

We use a small number of providers to run the service. Each only receives what it needs:

ProviderWhat it receivesWhy
Supabase Account details and your study data Sign-in and storage, so your data follows you between devices
Anthropic Timetable images you import; subject, textbook and lesson text Generating study plans and reading timetables
Open-Meteo Approximate coordinates only (no account details) Current weather for the clock widget
Expo (EAS) App version and device platform Delivering app updates

Notifications

Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device. Their contents are not sent to us or anyone else to deliver them.

Deleting your data

Open Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your sign-in, your profile and all study data — timetable, sessions, reminders and study plans. It cannot be undone, and we keep no copy afterwards.

You can also delete individual items at any time inside the app. Signing out clears the data held on that device without touching your account.

How long we keep things

Your data stays until you delete it, either item by item or by deleting your account. Imported images are never retained beyond the request that processes them.

Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app, export your planner data from the Profile screen, and delete your account outright. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to request a copy of your data or object to how it is handled. Contact us and we will help.

Children

MyStudyFlow is made for secondary school students. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are under the age at which you can consent on your own in your country, please get a parent or guardian's permission first. If you believe a child has given us data without that consent, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Your sign-in session is held in your device's secure storage. Access to stored data is restricted so that an account can only ever read and write its own rows.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page, and note anything significant in the app.
